# Plateful — App Store, Google Play and website copy (2026-10-28)

The story no competitor tells: **the cheapest store near you, or a price match at the till — and a ledger of what you actually saved.** Lead with it everywhere. Never quote an "average user saves $X" figure until real usage data exists; quote the shopper's own projection ("see what your usual shop could save").

- **Subtitle (30):** Cheapest store or price match

- **Keywords (100):** grocery,flyer,price match,meal plan,recipes,shopping list,Calgary,deals,budget,pantry,Superstore
- **Description:**

  Dinner, decided by this week's prices.

  Plateful reads this week's Calgary grocery flyers and what's already in your fridge, and ranks dinners you can cook tonight. Then it answers the question no recipe app does: where should you buy the rest?

  CHEAPEST STORE NEAR YOU — OR A PRICE MATCH AT THE TILL
  For every list, Plateful compares two routes: the single cheapest store within your radius, or a store that price-matches competitor flyers. Pick the price match and At the till lays out every competitor ad, ready to show the cashier.

  SEE WHAT YOU ACTUALLY SAVED
  Every price match, every list moved to the cheaper store and every sale item you buy goes into your savings ledger — by month, by how you saved, line by line. Not an estimate: what you did.

  COOK WHAT YOU HAVE
  Tell Plateful what's in the fridge. Recipes show "You have everything" or "You need 3 more ingredients · $8.40". Use it up shows what to cook before it turns.

  COOK IT STEP BY STEP
  Recipes scale to your household, cook mode reads the timers out of each step, and your notes and reviews stay with the recipe.

  ALSO
  • 28 cuisines, every dish photographed
  • A weekly plan and a grocery budget
  • Link a recipe from any site, TikTok or YouTube and Plateful prices it too
  • Your loyalty cards as barcodes at the till
  • Scan a shelf tag to see the lowest price nearby (preview)

  Plateful Plus — $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, first month free: price alerts, unlimited scans and imports, shared household list.
